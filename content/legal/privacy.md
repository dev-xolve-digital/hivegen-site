## What we collect

When you book a call, we collect the details you type into the booking form: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), the kind of business you run, how many videos you need each month, and a link to your footage (optional). We also note which page you booked from and the ad that brought you there.

## How we use it

We use your details only to arrange your call, send your calendar invite and reminders, and set up your free first edit. We don't sell your details.
