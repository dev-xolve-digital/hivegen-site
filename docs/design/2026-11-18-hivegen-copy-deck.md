## 09 · FAQ

1. **How fast will I get my video?**
   Usually within 24–48 hours of receiving your footage, depending on how complex the edit is. We confirm the timing before we start.
2. **What do I need to send?**
   Your raw footage through a Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link, plus your logo and any brand colors or fonts. If you have a past edit you love, send it too and we'll match the style. `[confirm]`
3. **Is it really white-label?**
   Yes. Your videos carry your branding or your client's, never ours: no HiveGen logo and no credits. `[confirm: add "we never contact your clients" if true]`
4. **What does Advanced AI add?**
   Everything in Premium, plus 3D text hooks pinned to the scene, illustrated animations, virtual staging of empty rooms, day-to-dusk and sky replacement, renovation previews and animated maps. We label AI-enhanced shots so your listings stay within MLS rules.
5. **How does the free first edit work?**
   Book a call, send footage from one listing, and we edit it free. It comes back with a small HiveGen watermark. If you want to use it, pay the normal price and we remove the watermark.
6. **How many revisions do I get?**
   One per video on Basic, two on Premium and Advanced AI. Single edits include one revision. `[confirm single-edit revisions]`
7. **Can I order just one video?**
   Yes. Single edits start at $90, depending on complexity. Monthly plans bring the price down to as little as $60 per video.
8. **What happens on the call?**
   Fifteen minutes. We look at your listings, agree on a style and your volume, and set up your free first edit. No pressure.
